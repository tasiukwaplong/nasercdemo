Executive Statement

Building a Self-Sustaining Power Sector for Nasarawa State

"Under the Nasarawa State Electricity Law 2024, our mission is clear: to establish a robust, transparent, and investor-friendly regulatory climate. By enforcing grid standards, streamlining licensing for off-grid mini-grids, and safeguarding consumer rights, NASERC is empowering local commerce and transforming livelihoods across Nasarawa State."
