Leadership Statement

Message from the Executive Chairman

"Our mission at NASERC is to build a modern, transparent, and sustainable electricity ecosystem in Nasarawa State that powers agricultural growth, drives industrialization, and guarantees reliable power for every household."

With the decentralization of electricity regulation under Nigeria's Electricity Act 2023 and the Nasarawa State Electricity Law 2024, the Commission is equipped to license off-grid generators, regulate distribution networks, and enforce cost-reflective yet equitable tariffs. We are committed to fostering investor confidence while rigorously protecting electricity consumers across all 13 Local Government Areas.

NASERC

The Nasarawa State Electricity Regulatory Commission (NASERC) is the statutory body empowered under the Nasarawa State Electricity Law 2024 to regulate power generation, distribution, mini-grids, and consumer rights across Nasarawa State.
