NASERC Hosts Stakeholder Summit on Off-Grid Power Generation Framework

August 4, 2026
Communications Unit
3 min read
Verified Official Announcement

The Chairman/CEO of the Nasarawa State Electricity Regulatory Commission (NASERC), Engr. Muhammad Bello, alongside Executive Commissioners and Directors, today hosted a high-level stakeholder consultation meeting with mini-grid developers, renewable energy investors, and key power sector representatives in Lafia.

The summit focused on evaluating Nasarawa State's 2026 Decentralized Electrification Strategy and outlining the operational parameters for the mandatory 100 kW licensing threshold framework enacted under the landmark Nasarawa State Electricity Law 2024.

"Nasarawa State is setting the benchmark for sub-national electricity regulation in Nigeria. Our regulatory framework is designed to eliminate bureaucratic bottlenecks for mini-grid investors while safeguarding consumers against illegal tariffs and poor quality of service."
— Engr. Muhammad Bello, Chairman/CEO of NASERC

Key Directives & Resolutions Reached

During the three-hour interactive session, the Commission ratified several key resolutions aimed at accelerating clean energy access across all 13 Local Government Areas in the state:

  • Fast-Track Registration for Sub-100 kW Projects: Off-grid solar mini-grids with an installed capacity up to 100 kW can now obtain operational permits within 10 business days via the NASERC Secretariat.
  • Full Regulatory Licensing (>100 kW): Mini-grid utility developments exceeding 100 kW capacity will undergo technical grid compatibility assessments, environmental impact reviews, and formal licensing approval.
  • Uniform Tariff Benchmarks: Tariff structures across commercial clusters and rural agricultural hubs will be subjected to NASERC Multi-Year Tariff Order (MYTO) cost-reflective standards.

The Commission reaffirmed its dedication to collaborating with distribution companies (DisCos) and mini-grid operators to ensure uninterrupted power supply, foster rural economic development, and establish Nasarawa State as the premier hub for sustainable off-grid renewable energy investments in Nigeria.
