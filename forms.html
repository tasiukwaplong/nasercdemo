Customer Complaint & Dispute Desk

Before lodging an appeal with NASERC, consumers must complete the statutory 3-step dispute resolution process. Use our interactive screening below to check your eligibility.

Statutory Dispute Handling Protocol

The 3-Step Customer Complaint Process

Important Rule: You cannot jump straight to NASERC. You must start at Step 1.
1
Step 1: DISCO

DisCo Customer Care (CCU)

Lodge your initial dispute with your electricity provider (AEDC or licensed Mini-Grid). The CCU investigates and issues a resolution.

Decision: Satisfied with DisCo?
• YES → DisCo notifies NASERC → END
• NO → Proceed to Step 2
2
Step 2: FORUM

Forum Office Appeal

If unsatisfied with the DisCo's ruling or if they exceed 15 days, appeal to the Electricity Consumer Forum Office (Lafia / Zonal Forum).

Decision: Satisfied with Forum?
• YES → END
• NO → Proceed to Step 3
3
Step 3: NASERC

NASERC Adjudication

Appeal to the Commission for final regulatory determination. NASERC reviews the docket and issues a binding decision.

Decision: Satisfied with NASERC?
• YES → END (Final)
• NO → Seek Court Remedies
