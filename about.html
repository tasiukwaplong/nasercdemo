About NASERC

Establishing statutory regulatory standards for power generation, distribution, mini-grids, and consumer rights in Nasarawa State.

Statutory Framework

Our Mandate & Legal Powers

Established under the Nasarawa State Electricity Law 2024 to regulate the state's intrastate electricity market.

Licensing & Permitting

Granting, renewing, and enforcing operational licenses for Mini-Grids, Independent Power Producers (IPPs), Embedded Generators, and Electricity Distribution Licensees across Nasarawa State.

Tariff Determination

Formulating equitable Multi-Year Tariff Orders (MYTO) to guarantee cost-reflective returns for infrastructure investors while protecting consumers against arbitrary rate hikes.

Consumer Advocacy

Establishing binding Customer Service Standards, managing fast-track dispute resolution mechanisms, and enforcing mandatory smart metering schedules for all DisCos.

Strategic Orientation

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To transform Nasarawa State into Nigeria's benchmark sub-national energy market—characterized by 100% universal electricity access, clean renewable energy integration, and world-class regulatory efficiency.

Our Mission

To create an enabling regulatory environment through fair rate-setting, robust licensing standards, rigorous grid compliance, and proactive consumer protection across all 13 Local Government Areas.

Core Institutional Values

Integrity Transparency Equity & Fairness Innovation Sustainability
