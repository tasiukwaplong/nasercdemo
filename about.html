About the Commission

Establishing a sustainable, competitive, and consumer-centric electricity market in Nasarawa State.

Our Statutory Mandate

Licensing & Permits

Granting, renewing, and supervising licenses for power generation, transmission, distribution, mini-grid operators, and meter asset providers across Nasarawa State.

Grid Standards Oversight

Enforcing safety protocols, technical performance benchmarks, power quality standards, and sub-station interconnectivity guidelines.

Consumer Protection

Resolving billing disputes, safeguarding consumers against wrongful service disconnections, and investigating meter installation complaints.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To transform Nasarawa State into a premier hub for sustainable off-grid renewable energy and reliable power distribution across Nigeria.

Our Mission

To formulate clear regulatory frameworks, encourage private sector mini-grid investments, and guarantee equitable service delivery for all consumers.

NASERC Core Values

INTEGRITY

Uphold honesty, ethical conduct, and impartiality

TRANSPARENCY

Operate openly, ensuring accountability and public confidence

PROFESSIONALISM

Deliver services with competence, excellence, diligence, and respect

INDEPENDENCE

Make objective and evidence-based regulatory decisions

INNOVATION

Adopt modern technologies and forward-looking solutions

CONSUMER PROTECTION

Protect consumer rights while promoting quality, reliability, and affordability

COLLABORATION

Foster productive partnerships with government, licensees, operators, communities, and stakeholders

SUSTAINABILITY

Encourage investments and regulatory practices that support long-term energy security and environmental sustainability

NASERC

The Nasarawa State Electricity Regulatory Commission (NASERC) regulates power generation, transmission, distribution, mini-grids, and consumer rights across Nasarawa State.
